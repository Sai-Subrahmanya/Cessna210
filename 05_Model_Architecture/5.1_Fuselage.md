# 5.1 Fuselage

## Overview

The fuselage is the primary structural body of the aircraft and serves as the central component around which all other aircraft systems are organized.

Within the completed model, the fuselage establishes the overall aircraft length and provides attachment locations for the wing, tail surfaces, and propeller system.

---

## Functions

- Defines aircraft body shape
- Connects major aircraft components
- Establishes overall aircraft dimensions
- Supports tail and wing integration
