# 5.2 Wing

## Overview

The wing is the primary lifting surface of the aircraft and one of the most significant geometric components within the model.

The wing geometry is defined through span, chord lengths, taper ratio, sweep, and dihedral parameters.

---

## Functions

- Generates lift
- Influences aircraft stability
- Defines aircraft span
- Determines wing planform geometry
