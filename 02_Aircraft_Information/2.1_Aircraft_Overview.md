# 2.1 Aircraft Overview

## Introduction

The Cessna 210 is a single-engine fixed-wing aircraft designed for personal, utility, and business aviation applications.

The aircraft belongs to the family of high-wing general aviation aircraft and is known for its versatility, reliability, and practical performance characteristics.

The aircraft configuration makes it an excellent candidate for aircraft geometry modeling because it contains all major components commonly found in conventional fixed-wing aircraft.

---

## Aircraft Category

### Aircraft Type

Single-Engine Fixed-Wing Aircraft

### Configuration

* Tractor Propeller Configuration
* Conventional Tail Configuration
* High Wing Design

### Primary Applications

* Personal Transportation
* Utility Operations
* General Aviation
* Pilot Training

---

## Why This Aircraft Is Suitable For Modeling

The aircraft contains all major geometry elements typically encountered during aircraft design:

* Fuselage
* Main Wing
* Horizontal Tail
* Vertical Tail
* Propeller

This makes it well suited for demonstrating aircraft geometry development using OpenVSP.
