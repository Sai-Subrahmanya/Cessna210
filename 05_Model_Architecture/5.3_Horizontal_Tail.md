# 5.3 Horizontal Tail

## Overview

The horizontal tail provides longitudinal stability and contributes to aircraft trim and balance.

The geometry consists of a stabilizer surface positioned at the rear of the aircraft.

---

## Functions

- Provides pitch stability
- Assists trim control
- Contributes to aircraft balance
