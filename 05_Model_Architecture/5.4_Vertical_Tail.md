# 5.4 Vertical Tail

## Overview

The vertical tail provides directional stability and helps maintain aircraft heading during flight.

It is mounted at the rear of the fuselage and works together with the horizontal tail.

---

## Functions

- Provides yaw stability
- Improves directional control
- Maintains aircraft alignment
