# 2.2 Aircraft Components

## Overview

The completed aircraft model consists of five major components.

Understanding the role of each component helps when reviewing the aircraft geometry and dimensional analysis sections later in the repository.

---

## Fuselage

The fuselage is the main body of the aircraft.

Functions:

* Connects all major aircraft components
* Provides structural support
* Defines overall aircraft length and shape

---

## Main Wing

The wing is the primary lifting surface.

Functions:

* Generates lift
* Influences aircraft stability
* Defines aircraft span and planform geometry

---

## Horizontal Tail

The horizontal tail contributes to longitudinal stability.

Functions:

* Pitch stability
* Trim control
* Aircraft balance

---

## Vertical Tail

The vertical tail contributes to directional stability.

Functions:

* Yaw stability
* Directional control
* Aircraft alignment

---

## Propeller

The propeller provides propulsion.

Functions:

* Generate thrust
* Drive forward motion
* Influence aircraft performance
